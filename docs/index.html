How it plays

Steer. Leap. Rise.

Drag to steer, tap to leapNo lanes: the climber goes exactly where your thumb goes. Tap to leap fallen trees and crevasses, and to dyno across blank rock.
Your rope is your lifeSteer through anchor rings to clip in. A clipped rope catches one fall. Fall without it and the climb is over.
A climb that never repeatsCliffside trails, sheer walls, ice-cave slides, vertical shafts, rope bridges, summits and paraglider flights to the next mountain.
Nine worlds, real campsEvery summit unlocks a camp to start from. Earn three stars in each world: the summit, its crystals and a climb without a fall.
Feel every stepA natural soundscape of wind, stone, ice and echoing caves with rich haptics. No music, just the mountain.
Yours, offlineNo ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no personal data. Plays anywhere, no Wi-Fi needed.
