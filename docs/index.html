How it plays

Swipe. Climb. Rise.

One-thumb controlsSwipe left or right to switch lanes, up to jump and leap between holds, down to duck and slide.
A run that never stays the sameRidges, cliff walls, mine-cart caves, rope bridges, shafts, summits and ziplines between peaks.
Seven worldsDawn foothills, amber canyon, crystal hollows, frost ridge, aurora peaks, the sky summit and the edge of space.
Missions & climbersComplete missions to rank up, keep a daily streak and unlock eight climbers with crystals.
Feel every stepA natural soundscape of wind, stone and echoing caves with rich haptics. No music, just the mountain.
Yours, offlineNo ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collected. Plays anywhere, no Wi-Fi needed.
