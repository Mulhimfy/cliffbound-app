Privacy policy

In short: Cliffbound does not collect, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

This policy explains how Cliffbound: Mountain Climbing (the "app"), a game for Android and iOS published by Mulhimfy ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to every version of the app, including those on Google Play, the App Store and our website.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal information. It does not send information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your progress: best altitude, crystals, summits reached and daily streak;
  • the climbers you unlocked and the one you play with;
  • your missions and rank;
  • your settings: sound, vibration and graphics quality.

This data never leaves your device through the app. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, clear its data, or choose Settings → Reset progress. Your phone's own backup service (Google or iCloud backup) may include it in backups that you control; we have no access to those.

Permissions

On Android the app uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings) and standard network access, which the app's web engine requires; the game itself works fully offline and makes no network requests during play. The app keeps the screen awake while you climb. It requests no other permissions.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any. If you download the app from Google Play or the App Store, those stores handle the download under their own privacy policies.

Links

The Settings screen links to this page and to our support page. These open in your browser, which applies its own privacy practices.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes would also be described in the app's release notes.
