Help & support

Something not working, an idea, or a summit you are proud of? Email ahmeddevyfy@gmail.com and tell us your phone model.

How do I play?

Swipe left/right to switch lanes, up to jump (or leap between holds on a cliff), and down to duck and slide (or press flat against the rock). Swipe down in the air to drop fast. The same swipes work on ridges, cliffs, mine carts, bridges and ziplines.

What is the score?

Your altitude in metres above camp. Every summit takes you across a zipline to a new world with its own sky and hazards.

What do crystals do?

Spend them on a second wind after a fall (the price rises within one climb) or to unlock new climbers. Missions and the daily climb pay bonus crystals.

What is the daily climb?

One mountain per day, the same for everyone. Climb it on consecutive days to grow your streak and your first-climb bonus.

The game feels slow on my phone

Open Settings → Graphics and choose Saver. The game also lowers its resolution automatically on slower phones. Closing other apps helps too.

There is no sound or vibration

Check Settings → Sound / Vibration, your phone's media volume and silent mode. On iPhone, haptics follow the system haptics setting.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device after every climb. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. There are no accounts, so progress cannot move between devices.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
