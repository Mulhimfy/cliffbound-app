Cliffbound 1.3: two new worlds, every world its own, a climber who really climbs.

- Two new worlds: Jade Falls, jungle cliffs with waterfalls, streams to leap and glowing grottoes, and Ember Caldera, a volcano at dusk with lava rivers, lava bombs and fire hawks. Nine worlds in all.
- Every world has its own hazards: mossy granite, sandstone and tumbleweeds, geodes, glowing basalt, giant snowballs, prayer-flag poles, asteroids and meteors. Caves are different in every world too, from mossy grottoes to lava tubes and aurora ice.
- A creature in every world instead of one eagle: a golden eagle, a vulture, a macaw, a crystal moth, a fire hawk, a snowy owl, a spirit owl, a chough and a star manta. They all have faces now, and they look angry.
- New running and climbing: a real running stride, and on walls the climber goes hand over hand with two ice axes, ice chips flying off every placement.
- The ice cave into the shaft is smooth now. The bend no longer goes black, daylight grows as you climb, and you come out over the lip level.
- Fairer stakes: two rope anchors per world, at most one amulet per world, and two continues per climb that cost more in higher worlds.
- Reminders: the game asks after your milestones while they are off. If your phone has blocked them, one tap opens its settings.
- Your progress carries over: every world keeps its stars and camps.

Install: download `Cliffbound.apk` below on an Android phone and open it (allow installs from your browser if asked).
