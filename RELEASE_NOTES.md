Cliffbound 1.2: evening reminders, a real tutorial, a new masked climber.

- Evening reminders with pictures, at most one a day around 9 pm (they learn when you usually climb): a new daily climb, a streak about to break, the summit you're closest to. Tap one to land on that screen. Switch them off in Settings.
- Daily climb stats: see how many climbers took today's mountain and where you stand ("higher than 68% of them"). Anonymous, with a switch in Settings.
- A guided tutorial that teaches every move, from steering to ice shafts. Falls in it are free: you rewind and try again. Replay it any time from Settings.
- A new look under the hood: a smooth mask and a single visor light, with no face.
- Tap any world, even a locked one, for a preview of what waits there.
- One missions button instead of two.

Install: download `Cliffbound.apk` below on an Android phone and open it (allow installs from your browser if asked).
